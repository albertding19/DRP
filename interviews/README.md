Thematic Analysis · Distinctive Contribution · Connection to Locked Scope · Persona

## How to use these files

- **For the M1 pitch (22 May):** mine the *Pitch-Ready Evidence* sections — every file has 2-4 short quotes/observations selected for the 3-min pitch. The 29yr's unprompted HMW articulation, the 60+'s "7 years is a joke," the MHP's "you recognise someone needs help but are unable to provide it," and the Nurse's "3-5 years" are the strongest candidates.
- **For persona drafting (before M2):** the *Persona Signals* sections give you 4-6 ready-made attributes per archetype. The data clusters cleanly into 3-4 persona archetypes — late-self-identifier worker, late-life-diagnosis adult, self-identified student, university-route diagnosed student — plus a stakeholder card for NHS practitioners.
- **For design ideation:** the *Design Implications* sections converge on a small set of hard constraints — no full feeds, no habit-tracker-guilt mechanics, no requirement to disclose at work, must work when medication fails, must accommodate a severity spectrum, must complement (not compete with) the NHS.
- **For the HCD Portfolio (Documentation, 80% weight):** these per-respondent analyses *are* the empathy-evidence the rubric asks for. Cite them as evidence that the team understood the target audience and stakeholder groups in depth.
